Statute · rule set owner-1 · 09 oct 2026

The rules the agent can't move.

8 clauses · in force · demo data

This set governs every proposed purchase. The AI may propose — it has one tool and no money. Only these rules, and then the owner (you), decide. The rules are read fresh for every evaluation, and none of them can be overridden by a model.

§ 1

Standing

Every request is evaluated against this rule set at proposal, again at approval, and again immediately before execution. A request that has no decision yet is simply not spent.

rule set owner-1 · USDevaluated at PROPOSAL · APPROVAL · PRE_EXECUTIONunreadable rules or state → fails closed, no spend
§ 2

A valid request

Malformed, missing, or ambiguous requests are refused at the boundary — before any rule is applied, a garbage request is never permission.

invalid request → deny
§ 3

Amount and currency

Amounts are positive integers in the currency's minor units; only USD is accepted — the MVP performs no exchange. Zero or negative amounts have no standing.

USD onlyamount ≤ 0 → denycurrency mismatch → deny
§ 4

Permitted categories

Only categories written here may be paid. An empty list permits nothing — the rule set fails safe by construction.

software · booksoutside the list → deny
§ 5

The monthly cap

A hard ceiling of 100.00 on verified captures for the month. It is checked before any approval condition, so no approval — human or otherwise — can override it.

cap 100.00 · hardcounted from verified captures onlywould exceed the cap → deny
§ 6

The per-purchase line

At or below the automatic limit, with everything else in order, no human is needed. Above it, a human is asked — the engine never waives its own line.

auto-allow ≤ 50.00above 50.00 → ask
§ 7

First-time payees

A payee we have not paid before goes to a human, every time — the unknown destination is exactly what the gate exists to slow down.

new payee → ask
§ 8

Otherwise

Everything that passes all of the above is allowed by the engine and may execute automatically under the vault-once card.

else → allow · within limits
